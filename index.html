{/* 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY */}

1. Executive Summary

Research Objective

This study evaluates technology adoption, distribution reliance, pricing practices, and revenue management mechanisms across independent small and mid-sized hotels in India.

Core Market Problem

While basic front-desk tools are gradually spreading, small and mid-sized independent hotels operate with fragmented data silos, fixed non-yield room tariffs, high OTA commission friction, and zero predictive analytics.

Key Findings & Industry Gap

  • PMS Fragmented Penetration: Roughly 48% of small budget properties lack cloud PMS infrastructure, relying instead on paper registers or basic spreadsheets.
  • Fixed Pricing Vulnerability: Budget properties overwhelmingly use static room rates regardless of surging weekend demand or local events.
  • High OTA Slippage: Heavy reliance on OTAs (MakeMyTrip/Goibibo charging 15-30% commissions) severely drains room GOPPAR.
  • The Intelligence Gap: Enterprise chains are adopting AI yield management (60% investment plan), while independent budget properties remain at 0% AI adoption.
